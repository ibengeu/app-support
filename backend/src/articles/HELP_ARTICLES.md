# Payment Support Help Center

Customer help articles for the company's payment and transfer service. The project does not specify a company name, contact channel, transfer timing policy, or refund policy, so these articles avoid making promises about those details.

## Find your payment reference

Your payment reference identifies a payment or transfer in support requests. It may look like `PAY-10041`.

Look for the reference in the payment confirmation or transaction details shown by the application. Include it when you contact support. Do not share your password, one-time passcode, or full card or account credentials with anyone claiming to be support.

If you cannot find a reference, share the approximate date and time, amount, and recipient details requested through the company's approved support channel. Share only the information needed to identify the transaction.

## Check a payment or transfer status

Contact support with your payment reference to ask about a transaction. Support can check the available transaction record and payment logs.

- **Successful:** The available record shows that the payment was marked successful. If the recipient has not received it, contact support with the reference so the transfer can be investigated.
- **Failed:** The available record shows that the payment failed. Check the failure reason, correct any details you control, and contact support if the reason is unclear.
- **No status or no matching record:** Check that you have the right reference. If the status remains unclear, contact support for a review.

Status information available to support may not show when another bank or service will display the funds.

## A transfer failed because of a gateway timeout

A gateway timeout means the payment service did not receive a timely response from the payment gateway. The status should be checked before you try the payment again, because the available information may not establish whether the receiving service processed it.

Contact support with your payment reference if the status is unclear or the amount appears to have been deducted. Do not assume the payment was completed or reversed until the transaction has been checked.

## A transfer failed because of insufficient funds

An insufficient-funds error means the payment could not proceed because the available balance did not cover it at the time of the check.

Check your available balance and any applicable transaction costs, then review the payment details. If you believe the balance was sufficient, contact support with the payment reference. Do not send account passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes.

## A transfer failed because the account number was invalid

An invalid-account-number error means the payment gateway rejected the account number provided for the recipient.

Check the recipient's account number and other transfer details with the recipient, then contact support if the information is correct but the transfer still fails. Do not ask the recipient to send sensitive banking credentials.

## The recipient has not received a successful transfer

If your transaction record shows success but the recipient cannot see the funds, contact support with the payment reference and the approximate transfer time. Support can compare the available transaction record with payment logs.

The successful status alone may not confirm when the recipient's bank or service will display the funds. Ask the recipient to check with their bank or service if appropriate. Contact support if the transfer remains unresolved.

## Contact support about a payment

Provide the payment reference and a brief description of the issue. If you do not have the reference, provide the approximate date and time and the amount using the company's approved support channel.

Never include your password, PIN, one-time passcode, access token, or full payment credentials in a support request. Support may ask for information needed to identify the transaction, but should not need your login secrets.
